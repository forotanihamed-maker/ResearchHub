# ResearchHub — Business Model

## Customer

Primary customer hypothesis:
- university faculty
- university
- research center

Initial go-to-market focus is faculty-level adoption rather than immediate university-wide deployment.

## Users

- students
- professors
- faculty administrators

## Core Offering

ResearchHub is delivered as a software service that can include:

- system access
- project management
- project collaboration
- file/document management
- faculty monitoring/dashboard
- deployment/setup
- technical maintenance
- user support
- product improvement
- periodic reporting

## Revenue Hypothesis

Current business-plan hypothesis is subscription-based pricing at faculty level, potentially annual or quarterly.

Exact pricing is intentionally not finalized before validation.

## Buyer Value

The strongest buyer-facing value proposition should be framed around **faculty project management infrastructure**, not simply professor-student chat.

Potential value:
- unified project inventory
- project status visibility
- structured execution
- document continuity
- reduced fragmentation
- faculty-level reporting

## Current Validation Gap

There is not yet enough real customer evidence to claim product-market fit or willingness to pay.

The next meaningful validation milestone is a real pilot, not adding more features.
