# ResearchHub — Industry Liaison Vision

**Status: Future / Not for current implementation**

## Vision

A future ResearchHub workflow may connect the university's industry liaison office with project execution.

```text
Industry need
    ↓
University industry liaison office
    ↓
ResearchHub project
    ↓
Professor / research team
    ↓
Execution + progress + communication
    ↓
Final report / deliverable
```

## Boundary

ResearchHub should not become the legal/financial party to an industry contract.

Contracts, payments, signatures and formal university obligations remain with the university's official office.

ResearchHub acts as the execution, coordination and documentation layer.

## Timing

This should be considered only after the core professor/student pilot has demonstrated real usage.
